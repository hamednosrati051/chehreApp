# Fonts bundled in the app

- Body/UI text: **Samim** (Regular + Bold, plain variant with Latin
  digits) — https://github.com/rastikerdar/samim-font, licensed under
  the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Free for commercial use, including
  bundling with software. Switched 2026-10-27 at Hamed's order after he
  found Estedad too thin and bad-looking.
- Display/headline/titleLarge: **Lalezar** (Regular).

## History

- Vazirmatn (v18) → Estedad (v19–v22). Estedad was dropped 2026-10-27:
  Hamed called it too thin and bad.
- B Nazanin was previewed 2026-10-27 but NOT chosen: classic serif
  (formal, not like the sample app), Regular only, and its download
  page labels it "free for personal use" (risky for a Bazaar release);
  it also lacks the em-dash glyph (renders as a box).
- IranSans was NEVER used: commercial (fontiran), only if Hamed
  supplies licensed files.
